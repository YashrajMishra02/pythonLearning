.       - Any Character Except New Line
\d      - Digit (0-9)
\D      - Not a Digit (0-9) (Capital letter negates w.r.t lower case letter)
\w      - Word Character (a-z, A-Z, 0-9, _)
\W      - Not a Word Character 
\s      - Whitespace (space, tab, newline)
\S      - Not Whitespace (space, tab, newline)

ANCHORS 


\b      - Word Boundary
\B      - Not a Word Boundary
^       - Beginning of a String (carrot symbol)
$       - End of a String
[]      - Matches Characters in brackets (only matches one character at a time and need "-" between the range i.e. a-z or A-Z or 0-9)
[^ ]    - Matches Characters NOT in brackets
|       - Either or
( )     - Group

Quantifiers:

*       - 0 or More
+       - 1 or More
?       - 0 or One
{3}     - Exact Number (exact amout of digits)
{3,4}   - Range of Numbers (Minimum, Maximum)

#### Sample Regexs ####

[a-zA-z0-9_.+-]+@[a-zA-Z0-9-]+\.[a-zA-Z0-9-.]+
